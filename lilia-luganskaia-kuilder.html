Lilia Luganskaia-Kuilder

Amsterdam-based artist and designer working across object, light, and space. Using glass and interactive light, she creates sculptural objects that shape perception and invite moments of pause. Her current collection, Ritual, explores focus, grounding, and radical slowing down.
