—Team

The people behind the ecosystem.

MADART does not derive its value from a system, a location or a format. It sits with the people who build it, the connections they make, and the choices they are willing to defend.

—Who does what

Justin Karijoredjo

Founder · Curatorial and Strategic Director

Architect of the MADART ecosystem. He devises the concept, makes the connections, and sets the direction. He decides which makers get a room, which partners get a seat at the table, and where the ecosystem goes next.

He has seen the commercial art world from the inside, and the curatorial choices come out of that experience rather than out of marketing.

Kimberly Tanikromo

Managing Director · Operations and Partnerships

Responsible for production and execution, commercial acquisition, and grant management for the Foundation. Her background in artist management turns intention into a working season.

She holds full power of attorney for Stichting The Bloys, the legal entity behind MADART Foundation.

Board

Stichting The Bloys is governed by a board of three, in office since February 2026 and jointly authorised: Summer Disbray (chair), Herns Jomes Agyemang-Duah (secretary) and Pim van Strien (treasurer). Board members receive no remuneration for their work as board members.
