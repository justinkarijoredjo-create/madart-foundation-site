02Practice

Lilia Luganskaia-Kuilder works between object, image and space. Her practice spans sculptural object design, photography, film and artist books, centred on material presence and quiet visual rhythm, and moves between modernist form and the relationship between nature and constructed environments.

Using glass and interactive light, she builds works that shape perception through narrative design. Ritual, her current body of work, investigates focus, grounding and radical slowing down through sculptural glass objects and interactive light. It premiered at Salone del Mobile.Milano in 2026.

The light in Ritual is custom-programmed and has only two states: a guided breathing sequence, and a steady glow when nothing is near it.

03Why this maker, and where

Luganskaia-Kuilder's authority sits in proportion rather than surface. Her glass is cut and graded according to Sogetsu ikebana, a discipline built on ratio, not decoration. That logic does not stay confined to a plinth: in a room built for living, an object proportioned for stillness needs no translating from gallery to house, because it was never separated from use in the first place.

The light piece paces itself against the body — ten minutes, motion-activated, indifferent to whether anyone is watching. The mirror holds a threshold rather than a reflection of taste. The ikebana table arrives unfinished by design: a stage, not a statement, waiting on whoever enters the room to complete it, arrangement by arrangement, day by day.

A maker's method proves itself differently once it has to hold its ground inside a domestic register — tested not by how it reads on a wall, but by how it behaves once someone sits down in front of it.
