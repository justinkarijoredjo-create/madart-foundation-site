Joost de Munnik & Ruud van den Eijnden

A furniture maker and a mechanism specialist build an entrance piece around an inherited clock movement. Oak, walnut and ceramic are cut to the rhythm the brass keeps; a bonsai above holds a slower measure against it. Presented by SPoT Den Haag, guest at Maison d'Artiste.

· Message Joost directly

Sales enquiries for this work are coordinated by Karel Bodegom at SPoT Den Haag — not by MADART Foundation. The piece is made to commission. 100% of the sale proceeds go to the artists.
