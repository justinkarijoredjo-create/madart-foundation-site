Season 01 — PARADOX

Where opposites no longer hold.

Season 01 ran at NIO House Amsterdam from April to July 2026 under the title PARADOX: work that holds two things at once without resolving them.

Documentation of this season is being assembled. Installation photography, the maker list and the wall texts will be published here.
