—Mission

The system looked away. We keep the record.

The median Dutch visual artist earns €16,000 a year — the lowest of any creative category, and below the social minimum of €18,800. More than one in four artists lives structurally below subsistence level. (CBS Monitor Kunstenaars, 2025)

The overlooked maker

The system did not overlook these makers because their work falls short. It overlooked them because it is too narrow to contain them. Four patterns come up again and again.

—Four patterns

01 The autodidact No recognised diploma, and so no official starting point for funds that require institutional credentials.
02 The uncategorisable Working between disciplines. The work falls just outside every recognisable box.
03 The too-versatile maker A practice too broad for the institutional expectation of a single, consistent signature.
04 The experienced outsider Decades of work, never institutional recognition. Quality has no expiry date.

How we select — The Dimensions

Every maker, every work and every exhibition is tested against four conditions. Each one is necessary. A maker who meets three of the four is not curated.

—The four conditions

01 Future-oriented Points forward in content, aesthetics or material. Not nostalgic — direction-giving.
02 Joyful Activates a positive, connecting response in the viewer.
03 Optimistic Forward-looking and human-centred. Critical is welcome. Cynical is not.
04 Innovative Objectively new in form, medium, technique or material.

MADART Foundation finances the maker's exhibition: production, presentation, fee. 100% of the sale proceeds go to the maker. No exclusive contract. No fee to take part.

—What MADART is not

Not an event agency

An event agency delivers logistics. We deliver context — a room that has meant something for three months before anyone walks in.

Not a gallery

No exclusive contracts and no commission inside our programme. Art here is an instrument for connection, not a revenue stream.

Not an agency

An agency adapts to the brief. We set the curatorial line and invite the partner to the table.

Not subsidy-dependent

The ecosystem is built to finance itself. Subsidies strengthen the Foundation; they are not a condition for its survival.

Not activism

We take no political or religious position. Selection is made on artistic and cultural grounds. Critical is welcome. Cynical is not.

Not a talent scheme

There is no age limit, no emerging bracket and no development track. There is work that counts, and a room to show it in.

How selection is recorded

Every assessment is written down: who applied, how we came across them, how the work tests against the four conditions, the outcome, and — where we decline — a short reason. A maker who is declined receives that reason and can apply again after twelve months.

The full policy, including how we handle conflicts of interest, is published on our governance page.
