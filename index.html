01What we do

We curate the makers the system overlooked, and give them a room in the middle of Amsterdam.

We finance the exhibition. The maker keeps 100% of every sale. No exclusive contract, no fee to take part — a room, an audience, and a fair deal.

The system that overlooks these makers is not broken by accident. Funds finance what institutions already show. Galleries show what already sells. Somewhere between the two, a whole category of serious work falls through — not because it isn't good enough, but because it doesn't fit the boxes the system was built around.

MADART Foundation exists for that gap. We cover production, presentation and a fee for the maker, on locations most of them could never reach on their own. In return we ask for nothing back: no commission, no exclusivity, no say over what they make next.
