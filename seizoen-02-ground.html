SEASON 023 July – 30 September 2026NIO House Amsterdam, fourth floor

The common ground shared by maker, Foundation and visitor alike. Not a stage built on favour. A foundation built on conviction.

MADART Foundation exists because we believe that good work deserves space, regardless of the path through which it came into being. We curate makers the system has not found. Not because their work falls short, but because the system is too narrow to contain them.

The people behind this season are the proof. Each of them has taken their own path. Each of them makes work that counts. That is enough.
