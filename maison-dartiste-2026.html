FESTIVAL17 – 19 September 2026 Within GLUE Amsterdam

Maison d'Artiste 2026

A festival by MADART Foundation

For three days the whole building opens. Eleven makers fill the sixth and seventh floors and the staircases; two floors below, Season 02 GROUND stays open with nineteen more. Thirty in all, across eight levels of the Metzgebouw, up to Rietveld’s glass dome on the roof — a room most people have never stood in.

Talks, tours, sound, a wine bar and a table where three makers sit down to say how they got here. Free to walk in, 10:00 – 18:00. A few things fill up: those are marked.

Where
Leidsestraat 32–34 — the Metzgebouw, now NIO House Amsterdam
When
17 – 19 September 2026, 10:00 – 18:00
Entry
Free. Walk in on any of the three days.
Floors
All eight levels are open, except while a programme item is running on them. See the building →
Booking
Most of the programme is open to anyone. The tours, the table talk, the workshop, the yoga and the sound sessions have limited places and need booking. Lunch is reserved separately.
Who sells what
Two things inside the festival are paid for. The Opening Night on Thursday is ticketed by MADART Foundation as an evening opening of its own exhibition; the production of that evening is bought in from Curated by MADART, the commercial organisation behind the Foundation, at market rate. Lunch is served and invoiced by Vermaat, and the Foundation receives no part of it. Everything else is free.

  3. 13:30 – 14:00 Second floor — the maker's presentation space

    The hospital: care or cure?

    Andreea Pirlica

    Andreea Pirlica →

    Hospital rooms have the reputation of being notoriously ugly, uncomfortable, and unwelcoming. As clinical efficiency has taken over patient-centered care, the hospital has turned into a techno-utopia, and care has turned into cure.

    Hospitals are designed to treat us, but are they designed to help us heal? Starting from my own experience as a patient, this lecture traces the history of the hospital room and brings together architectural analysis and patient experiences to explore how spaces of care have become spaces of cure — and what gets lost in between.

    In English

    Book — 15 places

  4. 14:00 – 14:45 Sixth floor

    MADART Table Talk

    Maaike Rabbinge, Loutje Hoekstra and Sandra Keja Planken, with Justin Karijoredjo

    Maaike Rabbinge studied psychology in Utrecht and political science in Amsterdam, became a circular designer, and now runs the collective ECHO. Loutje Hoekstra never went to an academy and taught herself everything she knows. Sandra Keja Planken spent a career as an interior architect before she began making autonomous work.

    Three makers who arrived here by three different routes, and not one of them the route the system recognises. Dutch funds date a career from a first presentation inside the established circuit. Galleries want a signature they can place. Both measure a path that none of these three walked — and neither has anything to do with the quality of the work.

    That is what MADART Foundation exists for, and it is easier to hear it from them than from us. They sit down at Ignore’s table and talk about how they got here, and about what they made for GROUND. Justin Karijoredjo leads the conversation.

    Forty-five minutes, standing room.

    Seated at a table by Ignore.

    Book — 30 places

    Colour in design

    Femke Hofhuis, Femm Amsterdam

    Colour as a design tool. Designer Femke Hofhuis, founder of Femm Amsterdam, on the power of colour and of how a space is experienced. How does colour shape the way we feel, and the way we read a room?

    And why are colour, light and art becoming more important in a world that keeps getting more digital? A talk about designing for the eye and for the feeling.

    In Dutch

    Book

  7. 16:00 – 17:00 Second floor

    Where neuroscience, behaviour, design and business meet

    Martijn den Otter, Neurofactor

    Martijn den Otter is a neuroscientist, profiler and founder of Neurofactor. As a co-researcher he was involved in the Mauritshuis study of Girl with a Pearl Earring, which used EEG, eye-tracking and fMRI to examine what art does inside the human brain.

    In his talk he brings those worlds together: what happens in the brain when we look at art, and how art, neurodesign and our surroundings shape the way we feel, think and function at work. With a live demonstration of a measurement.

    In Dutch

  3. 13:00 – 15:00 Seventh floor — the Rietveld dome

    Music experience and line art workshop

    Sami Abou Louh and Loutje Hoekstra

    Loutje Hoekstra →

    A musical experience and a workshop all around the subject of circles — circling sounds in a circular space, circling motions towards swirling line drawings that embrace fluidity and motion.

    Musician Sami Abou Louh opens the session with a live set crafted for the Rietveldkoepel, a circular space high above the city with sweeping views over Amsterdam. Participants are invited to listen and tune in, before Loutje Hoekstra, currently exhibiting at MADART Foundation but also known for her line drawings, leads a hands-on introduction to drawing as motion, flow and rhythm rather than structure.
