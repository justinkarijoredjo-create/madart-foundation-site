Who can take part

Anyone of eighteen or over entering on behalf of an organisation, with an organisation name, a role and a work email address. One entry per person. Staff of Curated by MADART and of MADART Foundation, and their immediate family, are excluded.

How the winner is chosen

At random, from all valid entries, by someone outside the campaign with no interest in its outcome. The winner is emailed within five working days. No reply within fourteen days, and we draw again.

Gambling tax

Dutch gambling tax falls due on prizes over €449. Curated by MADART pays it and files the return. The winner receives the prize in full and owes nothing.

Your details

Enter the draw and your business details go to Curated by MADART. Ask for news from MADART Foundation and those details go to the Foundation. The two sets are held separately and are never merged. Both are kept for two years unless you ask us to remove them sooner. See the privacy statement.

Complaints

Write to {{ site.email }} with “draw” in the subject line. We reply within five working days. If that does not settle it, the complaint can go to the Kansspelautoriteit, the Dutch gambling authority.
