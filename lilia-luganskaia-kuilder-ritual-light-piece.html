Ritual Light Piece

Gradient glass, metal, LED system · Ø 60 cm · 2026

MAISONDARTISTE-LL-02 · updated today

Ritual Light Piece is a sculptural glass object with an interactive meditation light that, when activated by motion and presence, emits a slow, breath-like rhythm guiding a 10-minute meditation cycle. Designed as a focus anchor for grounding and pause, the piece is custom-programmed with only two light states: a guided breathing cycle and a constant, steady glow when inactive.
