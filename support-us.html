—Support us

Buying a work is the most direct way to support a maker.

100% of a sale goes to the maker. If you would rather support the room itself — the production, the presentation and the fee that make a season possible — there are three ways.

—Three ways

Donate

Private donations pay for production, presentation and maker fees. Stichting The Bloys has applied for cultural ANBI status; until that is granted, donations are not tax deductible.

Talk to us about donating

Become a cultural partner

Brands and organisations can support the Foundation directly as a cultural partner. This is separate from a commercial partnership with Curated by MADART, and is recorded as such.

Get in touch

For funds and grant-makers

MADART Foundation operates through Stichting The Bloys. Our selection policy, governance and reporting are published in full.

Governance and published information

MADART Foundation is not built on the assumption that subsidy pays the bill. Curated by MADART, the commercial arm, structurally finances this Foundation. Subsidies and donations strengthen it; they are not a condition for its survival.
