Bertjan Pot — Dotty

Mirrors can be quite honest. Dotty introduces a layer of dots that merges with the reflected image, creating a subtle distraction and gently blurring reality. The interplay between a graphic grid and a realistic image is a recurring theme in Bertjan Pot's work. In Dotty, this dialogue takes on a new dimension: the mirror transforms the dotted pattern into a three-dimensional grid, while the realistic imagery is continuously shaped by whatever is reflected.

Boris de Beijer — Cassata Collezione

Cassata Collezione is a collection of handblown table-glass, inspired by glassware from classical antiquity and made using the same methods as the ancient Greeks and Romans. Its name is a flirtatious reference to the colorful Sicilian dessert, which, like the glassware, carries a history reaching back more than a millennium. Every piece is made using straightforward techniques that don't hide small flaws and imperfections — so each piece is unique.

Carole Baijings — Horizons

Always looking to rethink the way we interact with design, Carole Baijings created the Horizons salt & pepper set for Buro Berger. The minimal shape acts as a canvas for carefully selected translucent colors that form a horizon when the three pieces are placed together — inspired by her hand-made wooden color studies, each combination enhances the next.

Martin Hirth — Beln

Beln is a truly distinctive table lamp — a metal-bent beauty that unites a precise, hexagonal base with three softly curved lampshades. Its bonbon-like appearance is almost sculptural. Beln plays boldly with light and shadow, casting gentle reflections and graphic contrasts that shift throughout the day — a poetic fusion of geometry and softness.

Nynke Tynagel — Bluedot

The Bluedot Series is a contemporary take on traditional Delft Blue ware. Rather than familiar scenes of windmills, florals or ships, it focuses on pattern and rhythm — layered shades of blue building rhythmic compositions that echo the ornamental logic of Delft ceramics in a more abstract, contemporary language.
