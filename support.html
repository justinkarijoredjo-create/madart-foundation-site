02Give every yearThe route we recommend

A periodic gift is a commitment for five years, laid down in a written agreement between you and the Foundation. It is the most valuable form of support a small foundation can receive, because it lets us plan a season before we know what it will earn.

Under Dutch tax law a periodic gift to a cultural ANBI is not subject to the threshold that applies to ordinary gifts, and is deductible in full. For anyone who intends to give more than once, it is simply the better instrument — for you and for us.

Since 2014 no notary is involved. A private agreement with a legally valid digital signature is enough; if you have a tax partner, they co-sign. It takes minutes, and it is the one piece of paperwork worth doing.

{% if giving.anbi_granted %}

Cultural ANBI status granted. Private donors may deduct 125% of a gift to a cultural ANBI, up to €1,250 in additional deduction; companies 150%, up to €2,500. To put a number on it: a periodic gift of €1,000 a year returns roughly €470 through your tax return, against roughly €225 for an ordinary one-off gift. Your own situation decides what applies — we are not tax advisers.

{% else %}

Cultural ANBI status is applied for and not yet granted. Until it is, gifts to MADART Foundation are not tax deductible. We would rather say that plainly than let you find out afterwards. The agreement can be signed now and takes effect the moment the status is granted.

03Friend of MADART Foundation

A yearly gift, and a way to stay close to what the Foundation is doing.

Friend of MADART Foundation is a gift, not a ticket. What you receive in return is deliberately kept to what a gift may carry: a written account of the season, your name in the annual report if you want it there, and the first word on what is coming. No free admission, no discounts, no priority — those turn a gift into a purchase, and a purchase is neither deductible nor ours to sell here.
