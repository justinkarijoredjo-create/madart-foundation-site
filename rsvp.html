GLUE AMSTERDAM 2026 · 17–19 SEPTEMBER

Private Evenings

From six in the evening the fourth, sixth and seventh floors close to the public and open to a smaller room. Makers are present. Places are limited.

During the day the house is open to everyone: GROUND on the fourth floor and Maison d'Artiste on the sixth and seventh, one route through one building. The evenings are a different thing — fewer people, more time, and the chance to talk to the makers about work that is still on the wall behind them.

Friday 18 SeptemberPublic 10:00–18:00 · Talks from 15:00 · Private from 18:00
Saturday 19 SeptemberPublic 10:00–18:00 · Talks from 15:00 · Private from 18:00

NIO House Amsterdam, Leidsestraat 32-34. Talks on the second floor, made possible by Ignore and Charles & More. The Saturday talks programme is still being confirmed.
