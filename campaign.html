The fourth floor, for one evening.

Thirty makers, eight levels, three days. On Saturday at six the work comes down and this floor goes back to being a room in a building from 1891, with Rietveld’s glass dome three storeys above it. One evening on it can be yours.

MADART Foundation curates what hangs here. Curated by MADART hires out the room and pays for the programme. Two senders, two lists, kept apart.
