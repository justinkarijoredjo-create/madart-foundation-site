Devoonsounds

Amsterdam-based studio creating high-end sound sculptures — objects designed to be seen, heard and felt, not hidden away. Each piece is 3D-printed from recycled Carrara marble or hand-cast in eco-resin, then hand-finished in Amsterdam.
