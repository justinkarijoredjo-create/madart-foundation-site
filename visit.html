Getting there

The building sits on the corner of the Leidsestraat and the Keizersgracht. Trams 1, 2, 5, 11 and 12 stop at Koningsplein or Prinsengracht, both two minutes away. There is no parking at the building; the Q-Park under the Byzantium or the Kleine-Gartmanplantsoen garage are the nearest.

Take the lift to the fourth floor. If you are visiting during an anchor event, the sixth and seventh floors may also be open — the desk on the ground floor will tell you.
