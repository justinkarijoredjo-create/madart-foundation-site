SEASON 03 — INLINE · GALLERY 2

Aad Kruiswijk

Kruiswijk's work is made by a machine that can only do one thing, and it looks like something pulled from the earth. For INLINE, he has rebuilt his largest work into a functioning DJ booth — the same object that stood as sculpture during GROUND, now played, producing sound, with people standing behind it.
