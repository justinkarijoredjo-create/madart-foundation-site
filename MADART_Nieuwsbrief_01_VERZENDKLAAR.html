GLUE Amsterdam · 17–19 September 2026

The dome opens

Rietveld built a glass dome on the roof of the Metzgebouw in 1933. Le Corbusier and Mies van der Rohe showed there. For three days it opens again.

Eight floors. Thirty makers. Free entry, 10:00–18:00.
